# Privacy Policy – Thakkirni

- No account and no server. Tasks, ideas and settings are stored only on your phone.
- The microphone is used only while the listening screen is open. Speech is converted to text by the phone's built-in speech service (Apple or Google), which may process audio on their servers under their own policies. The app does not store recordings.
- Reminders are scheduled locally on the device.
- Delete any reminder by swiping it, or delete the app to remove all its data.
